## What the screen shows

After `RunStarted` the header reads `streaming step 0 of 21` while the loss chart is still empty.
After step 0 completes it reads the same `streaming step 0 of 21`. Two different states, one
wording.

## Why it costs

A user cannot tell from the state line whether the first step has finished; a slow first step looks
like a recorded step 0.

## Cost estimate

S: say "waiting for step 0" (or no progress) until the first step arrives.
