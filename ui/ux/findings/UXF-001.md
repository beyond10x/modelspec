## What the screen shows

Each parameter is a panel of two norm charts and two histograms, 961 px tall at 1366x768, stacked
below the loss chart. Only the section's step picker and the first parameter's name are in view
at the gating viewports; the first parameter's gradient norm chart starts below the fold, and the
second parameter of `two-layer-relu` is two pages down. The page has no list of its parameters.

## Why it costs

The screen-only user reads the gradient norm of the first parameter at glance 3 (not in view) at
every key moment and needs one `PageDown` at the end state. The coordinator's always-visible slot
"the parameter list" cannot be met as written: R-00 gates the parameter section's head instead
(`adapter.ts`, `ALWAYS_VISIBLE`).

## Cost estimate

M: a compact list of parameters with their latest norms in view, or a layout that puts the norms
beside the loss chart; a layout change, so a design decision of a later round.
