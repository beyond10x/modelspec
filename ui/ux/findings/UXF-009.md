## What the screen shows

A histogram has no count axis. A bin's count is in an SVG `<title>`, which a mouse user sees as a
tooltip after hovering one bar. The keyboard reaches no bar, and the accessibility tree carries the
edges and no counts.

## Why it costs

Anyone who needs a count (is that bin 1 or 10?) hovers bar by bar; a keyboard or screen-reader user
cannot get it at all.

## Cost estimate

S: a count axis (max count at the top), or the counts in the img's accessible description.
