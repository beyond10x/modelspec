## What the screen shows

In `linear-regression` the loss line falls to the bottom of the plot within the first few steps and
lies on the axis for the rest of the 500 steps; 11 orders of magnitude of progress are one flat line.
`two-layer-relu` shows the same shape after step 20. The y axis labels only its minimum and maximum.

## Why it costs

Whether the loss still falls, stalls or rises late in the run is the question the chart is there
for, and it cannot be read; the user falls back to the caption's single number.

## Cost estimate

M: a log value axis for the loss (switchable, or chosen when the range spans orders of magnitude).
